A release with Gex 64: Enter the Gecko and a fix to Super Mario 64. realsf2 converts the music of Nintendo 64 games to MIDI, SoundFont 2 and SFZ files.

| File | Contents |

Requires 64-bit Windows 10 or later, with no additional runtime DLLs. Drop one or more images or ROM sets onto `realsf2.exe`. You can also use a terminal. `realsf2 --help` lists the options. Output goes in a folder beside each image or set.

For macOS, follow the build instructions in the README. macOS builds include `Realsf2.app`. Drop images or ROM sets onto the app in Finder to convert them.

## Changes

- **Gex 64: Enter the Gecko.** realsf2 converts the 27 songs of the USA release. The game plays them on an early form of the N64 Music Tools. Each song plays at the volume and with the reverb that the game's code gives it. A song that a level fades in plays at the volume where the fade ends. The songs play at the game's default music volume. The game's options can raise it by 12 dB.
- **Super Mario 64.** The credits' sequence 0x1A plays at the volume of 85 that its script sets, where the game's table holds 70.
- **The libraries.** realsf2 now builds on ntdlib 3.7.0. It still builds on seqlib 1.4.0 and xsflib 1.10.0.
