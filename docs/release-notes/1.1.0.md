A release with the Aleck64 and fixes to two games. realsf2 now converts the music of seven games of Seta's Aleck64, an arcade board built from the Nintendo 64's chips, from their ROM sets. realsf2 converts the music of Nintendo 64 games to MIDI, SoundFont 2 and SFZ files.

Requires 64-bit Windows 10 or later, with no additional runtime DLLs. Drop one or more images or ROM sets onto `realsf2.exe`. You can also use a terminal. `realsf2 --help` lists the options. Output goes in a folder beside each image or set.

For macOS, follow the build instructions in the README. macOS builds include `Realsf2.app`. Drop images or ROM sets onto the app in Finder to convert them.

## Changes

- **The Aleck64.** realsf2 converts the music of Eleven Beat, Hi Pai Paradise, Mayjinsen 3, Star Soldier: Vanishing Earth, Super Real Mahjong VS, Tower & Shaft and Vivid Dolls. An Aleck64 game comes as the ROM set of its board, not as a cartridge image. Give realsf2 the set as a ZIP archive or as a folder of its chips. realsf2 finds each chip of the game by its SHA-1 and size, whatever the file is called, and puts the image together from them.
- **Super Real Mahjong VS in stereo.** The game plays its music in mono or in stereo, as a setting in its service menu picks. realsf2 converts both. Each stereo song has "(stereo)" after its name.
- **Zipped images.** A cartridge image may be in a ZIP archive, as the only file of the archive with the extension `.z64`, `.v64` or `.n64`.
- **Four games with streamed music.** Hanabi de Doon! - Don-chan Puzzle, Hi Pai Paradise 2, Kurukuru Fever and Magical Tetris Challenge play each song as one long recording. realsf2 says so and writes nothing for them.
- **Loop ends in the SDK's compressed sequences.** A loop end whose offset holds the byte 0xFE broke its track. Chameleon Twist 2's sequence 0x02 played one of its tracks past its loop.
- **Super Mario 64.** The merry-go-round's sequence 0x13 plays at the volume of 78 that the game gives it, where the game's table holds 65. Sequence 0x1F is named "Grand Star collected", for the scene that plays it.
- **The libraries.** realsf2 now builds on seqlib 1.3.0, xsflib 1.9.0 and ntdlib 3.4.0.

## Known limitations

As in 1.0.1, with one more for the Aleck64. A MIDI file holds 16 channels. Most of Hi Pai Paradise's songs have more, and two of their channels then share a track. Where their notes meet, they share the track's controllers. See "Differences from the game" in the README for the full list.
